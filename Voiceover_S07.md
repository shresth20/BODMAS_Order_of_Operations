## How to use this script (read before generating audio)

- **Audience:** primary-school maths learners. Keep the voice warm, friendly, and clear.
- **Pace convention:** durations are calculated at **140 words/minute** using
  `seconds ≈ round(words ÷ 2.33)`. **Recommendation:** for this young audience a
  slightly slower **~120 wpm** read often lands better — if you adopt it, multiply
  every duration below by **≈1.17** (total becomes ~5 min 30 s). Pick one pace and
  keep it consistent.
- **How to read the maths aloud** (apply everywhere — do *not* say symbol names):
  - `×` → "times" · `÷` → "divided by" · `+` → "plus" · `−` → "minus"
  - `( )` → "in brackets" / "inside the brackets"
  - `=` → "equals" / "becomes"
  - Example: `3 × 4 + 2` is read **"3 times 4 plus 2."**
- `*word*` = stress this word. `[pause]` = short beat (~0.5 s); add ~0.5 s to duration when present.
- Numbers are spoken as words (e.g. "twelve"), shown here as digits for brevity.
