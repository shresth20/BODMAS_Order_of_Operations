# Math Pattern Detectives — BODMAS Adventure

## Lesson Plan Mechanics and Sequence

### Lesson Focus

Students discover and apply the order of operations through a sequence of guided BODMAS rule investigations.

Core rules taught:

1. Multiplication before addition.
2. Multiplication before subtraction.
3. Division before addition and subtraction.
4. Addition and subtraction have equal priority and are solved left to right.
5. Multiplication and division have equal priority and are solved left to right.
6. Brackets are solved first.
7. Full BODMAS order: Brackets, Of, Division/Multiplication, Addition/Subtraction.

---

## Overall Lesson Sequence

| Step | Lesson Segment | Learning Mechanic |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | × Before + | Students identify multiplication as the first operation in mixed multiplication/addition expressions. |
| 2 | × Before + Practice | Students answer four practice questions using click-operation, rule-choice, step-solving, and worked-solution comparison. |
| 3 | × Before − | Students identify multiplication as the first operation in mixed multiplication/subtraction expressions. |
| 4 | × Before − Practice | Students answer four practice questions using the same practice structure. |
| 5 | ÷ Before + and − | Students identify division as the first operation when division appears with addition or subtraction. |
| 6 | ÷ Before + and − Practice | Students complete four practice questions on division priority. |
| 7 | + and − Equal Priority | Students learn that addition and subtraction are solved from left to right. |
| 8 | + and − Equal Priority Practice | Students complete four practice questions on left-to-right addition/subtraction. |
| 9 | × and ÷ Equal Priority | Students learn that multiplication and division are solved from left to right. |
| 10 | × and ÷ Equal Priority Practice | Students complete four practice questions on left-to-right multiplication/division. |
| 11 | Brackets First | Students learn that bracketed operations are solved before all other operations. |
| 12 | Brackets First Practice | Students complete four practice questions on brackets-first reasoning. |
| 13 | Grand Challenge | Students solve the same expression with and without brackets and compare how brackets change the answer. |
| 14 | BODMAS Ladder | Students arrange the BODMAS operation groups in the correct order. |
| 15 | Nested Brackets Challenge | Students solve expressions with brackets inside brackets by working from the innermost brackets outward. |
| 16 | Insert the Brackets | Students place brackets to make expressions reach target values. |
| 17 | Final BODMAS Review | Students review each rule, then order the steps of a full BODMAS expression. |

---

## Reusable Discovery-Level Mechanic

Each rule-discovery level follows the same instructional sequence:

1. Present a real-world context and expression.
2. Ask which operation should be solved first.
3. Student selects the first operation.
4. If the choice is incorrect, give a rule-based hint and let the student try again.
5. If the choice is correct, simplify that operation first.
6. Continue solving the reduced expression.
7. In selected rounds, compare the incorrect left-to-right or wrong-priority answer with the correct BODMAS answer.
8. Repeat across four examples so students notice the pattern.
9. Reveal the rule with worked examples.
10. Move to four practice questions.

# Level 1 — × Before +

## Introductory Scenario

A child buys 3 packets of crayons with 4 crayons each, and gets 2 extra crayons.

Expression: `3 × 4 + 2`

Prompt: How many crayons are there in all? Which part should be solved first?

## Target Rule

When an expression has multiplication and addition, solve multiplication first, then addition.

Example rule working:

```text
3 + 4 × 2
= 3 + 8
= 11
```

## Exploration Rounds

| Round | Expression | Correct First Action | Correct Working | Misconception Comparison |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `3 + 4 × 2` | Solve `4 × 2` first. | `4 × 2 = 8`; `3 + 8 = 11` | Left-to-right gives `3 + 4 = 7`; `7 × 2 = 14`. |
| 2 | `5 × 2 + 6` | Solve `5 × 2` first. | `5 × 2 = 10`; `10 + 6 = 16` | None. |
| 3 | `7 + 3 × 4` | Solve `3 × 4` first. | `3 × 4 = 12`; `7 + 12 = 19` | Left-to-right gives `7 + 3 = 10`; `10 × 4 = 40`. |
| 4 | `2 × 6 + 5` | Solve `2 × 6` first. | `2 × 6 = 12`; `12 + 5 = 17` | None. |

## Practice Sequence

| Question | Mechanic | Expression | Expected Response |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select first operator | `3 + 4 × 2` | Select `×`. |
| 2 | Choose matching rule | `7 + 2 × 5` | Choose `× Before +`. |
| 3 | Step solve | `5 + 3 × 4` | `3 × 4 = 12`; then `5 + 12 = 17`. |
| 4 | Choose correct worked solution | `6 + 2 × 3` | Choose the solution that does `2 × 3` first: `6 + 6 = 12`. |

A shopkeeper has 20 bananas. He sells 3 baskets with 4 bananas each.

Expression: `20 − 3 × 4`

Prompt: How many bananas are left? Which part should be solved first?

## Target Rule

When an expression has multiplication and subtraction, solve multiplication first, then subtraction.

Example rule working:

```text
10 − 3 × 2
```

|---:|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | `20 − 4 × 3` | Solve `4 × 3` first. | `4 × 3 = 12`; `20 − 12 = 8` | Left-to-right gives `20 − 4 = 16`; `16 × 3 = 48`. |
| 4 | `5 × 4 − 9` | Solve `5 × 4` first. | `5 × 4 = 20`; `20 − 9 = 11` | None. |

## Practice Sequence

| Question | Mechanic | Expression | Expected Response |

| 1 | Select first operator | `10 − 3 × 2` | Select `×`. |

# Level 3 — ÷ Before + and −

## Introductory Scenario

12 laddoos are shared equally among 3 children, and 5 more laddoos are added.

Expression: `12 ÷ 3 + 5`

Prompt: How many laddoos are there in total? Which part should be solved first?

## Target Rule

When an expression has division with addition or subtraction, solve division first.

Example rule working:

```text
10 + 12 ÷ 3
= 10 + 4
= 14

15 − 6 ÷ 2
= 15 − 3
= 12
```

## Exploration Rounds

| Round | Expression | Correct First Action | Correct Working | Misconception Comparison |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `6 + 9 ÷ 3` | Solve `9 ÷ 3` first. | `9 ÷ 3 = 3`; `6 + 3 = 9` | Left-to-right gives `6 + 9 = 15`; `15 ÷ 3 = 5`. |
| 2 | `10 ÷ 2 + 7` | Solve `10 ÷ 2` first. | `10 ÷ 2 = 5`; `5 + 7 = 12` | None. |
| 3 | `10 − 6 ÷ 2` | Solve `6 ÷ 2` first. | `6 ÷ 2 = 3`; `10 − 3 = 7` | Left-to-right gives `10 − 6 = 4`; `4 ÷ 2 = 2`. |
| 4 | `15 ÷ 3 − 2` | Solve `15 ÷ 3` first. | `15 ÷ 3 = 5`; `5 − 2 = 3` | None. |

## Practice Sequence

| Question | Mechanic | Expression | Expected Response |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select first operator | `8 + 6 ÷ 2` | Select `÷`. |
| 2 | Choose matching rule | `5 + 9 ÷ 3` | Choose `÷ Before +`. |
| 3 | Step solve | `4 + 10 ÷ 2` | `10 ÷ 2 = 5`; then `4 + 5 = 9`. |
| 4 | Choose correct worked solution | `12 + 8 ÷ 4` | Choose the solution that does `8 ÷ 4` first: `12 + 2 = 14`. |

# Level 4 — + and − Have Equal Priority

## Introductory Scenario

Riya has 10 stickers, gives away 4, then gets 3 more.

Expression: `10 − 4 + 3`

Prompt: How many stickers does Riya have? Which part should be solved first?

## Target Rule

Addition and subtraction have equal priority. When both appear, solve from left to right.

Example rule working:

```text
10 − 4 + 3
= 6 + 3
= 9

8 + 5 − 6
= 13 − 6
= 7
```

## Exploration Rounds

| Round | Expression | Correct First Action | Correct Working | Misconception Comparison |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `10 − 4 + 3` | Solve the leftmost operation: `10 − 4`. | `10 − 4 = 6`; `6 + 3 = 9` | Solving `4 + 3` first gives `10 − 7 = 3`. |
| 2 | `8 + 5 − 6` | Solve the leftmost operation: `8 + 5`. | `8 + 5 = 13`; `13 − 6 = 7` | None. |
| 3 | `15 − 3 + 5` | Solve the leftmost operation: `15 − 3`. | `15 − 3 = 12`; `12 + 5 = 17` | Solving `3 + 5` first gives `15 − 8 = 7`. |
| 4 | `9 + 7 − 4` | Solve the leftmost operation: `9 + 7`. | `9 + 7 = 16`; `16 − 4 = 12` | None. |

## Practice Sequence

| Question | Mechanic | Expression | Expected Response |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select first operator | `10 − 4 + 3` | Select the leftmost `−`. |
| 2 | Choose matching rule | `8 − 3 + 5` | Choose `Left to Right`. |
| 3 | Step solve | `9 − 2 + 4` | `9 − 2 = 7`; then `7 + 4 = 11`. |
| 4 | Choose correct worked solution | `10 − 3 + 5` | Choose the solution that solves left to right: `7 + 5 = 12`. |

# Level 5 — × and ÷ Have Equal Priority

## Introductory Scenario

12 chocolates are divided into 2 groups, then each group is packed into 3 boxes.

Expression: `12 ÷ 2 × 3`

Prompt: Which part should be solved first?

## Target Rule

Multiplication and division have equal priority. When both appear, solve from left to right.

Example rule working:

```text
12 ÷ 2 × 3
= 6 × 3
= 18

4 × 5 ÷ 2
= 20 ÷ 2
= 10
```

## Exploration Rounds

| Round | Expression | Correct First Action | Correct Working | Misconception Comparison |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `12 ÷ 2 × 3` | Solve the leftmost operation: `12 ÷ 2`. | `12 ÷ 2 = 6`; `6 × 3 = 18` | Solving `2 × 3` first gives `12 ÷ 6 = 2`. |
| 2 | `4 × 5 ÷ 2` | Solve the leftmost operation: `4 × 5`. | `4 × 5 = 20`; `20 ÷ 2 = 10` | None. |
| 3 | `30 ÷ 5 × 2` | Solve the leftmost operation: `30 ÷ 5`. | `30 ÷ 5 = 6`; `6 × 2 = 12` | Solving `5 × 2` first gives `30 ÷ 10 = 3`. |
| 4 | `6 × 4 ÷ 3` | Solve the leftmost operation: `6 × 4`. | `6 × 4 = 24`; `24 ÷ 3 = 8` | None. |

## Practice Sequence

| Question | Mechanic | Expression | Expected Response |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select first operator | `12 ÷ 4 × 3` | Select the leftmost `÷`. |
| 2 | Choose matching rule | `8 ÷ 2 × 3` | Choose `Left to Right`. |
| 3 | Step solve | `20 ÷ 4 × 3` | `20 ÷ 4 = 5`; then `5 × 3 = 15`. |
| 4 | Choose correct worked solution | `24 ÷ 4 × 2` | Choose the solution that solves left to right: `6 × 2 = 12`. |

# Level 6 — Brackets First

## Introductory Scenario

A teacher makes 3 groups. Each group has 4 boys and 2 girls.

Expression: `3 × (4 + 2)`

Prompt: How many students are there in all? Which part should be solved first?

## Target Rule

Brackets are solved before multiplication, division, addition, and subtraction.

Example rule working:

```text
(2 + 3) × 4
= 5 × 4
= 20

3 × (4 + 2)
= 3 × 6
= 18
```

## Exploration Rounds

| Round | Expression | Correct First Action | Correct Working | Misconception Comparison |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `(2 + 3) × 4` | Solve inside brackets: `2 + 3`. | `2 + 3 = 5`; `5 × 4 = 20` | Ignoring brackets gives `3 × 4 = 12`; `2 + 12 = 14`. |
| 2 | `3 × (4 + 2)` | Solve inside brackets: `4 + 2`. | `4 + 2 = 6`; `3 × 6 = 18` | None. |
| 3 | `(5 + 1) × 6` | Solve inside brackets: `5 + 1`. | `5 + 1 = 6`; `6 × 6 = 36` | Ignoring brackets gives `1 × 6 = 6`; `5 + 6 = 11`. |
| 4 | `2 × (7 + 3)` | Solve inside brackets: `7 + 3`. | `7 + 3 = 10`; `2 × 10 = 20` | None. |

## Practice Sequence

| Question | Mechanic | Expression | Expected Response |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select first operation | `(3 + 4) × 2` | Select `+` inside the brackets. |
| 2 | Choose matching rule | `(5 + 1) × 3` | Choose `( ) Brackets First`. |
| 3 | Step solve | `(2 + 5) × 4` | `2 + 5 = 7`; then `7 × 4 = 28`. |
| 4 | Choose correct worked solution | `(4 + 2) × 3` | Choose the solution that solves inside brackets first: `6 × 3 = 18`. |

# Slide 13 — Grand Challenge

## Purpose

Students compare how brackets change the result of expressions that otherwise use the same numbers and operations.

## Phase 1 — No Brackets

Expression: `5 + 2 × 3 − 4`

| Step | Correct Action | Resulting Expression |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Solve `2 × 3 = 6`. | `5 + 6 − 4` |
| 2 | Solve `5 + 6 = 11`. | `11 − 4` |
| 3 | Solve `11 − 4 = 7`. | Final answer: `7` |

## Phase 2 — With Brackets

Expression: `(5 + 2) × 3 − 4`

| Step | Correct Action | Resulting Expression |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Solve `(5 + 2) = 7`. | `7 × 3 − 4` |
| 2 | Solve `7 × 3 = 21`. | `21 − 4` |
| 3 | Solve `21 − 4 = 17`. | Final answer: `17` |

## Comparison Conclusion

`5 + 2 × 3 − 4 = 7`

`(5 + 2) × 3 − 4 = 17`

The brackets change the first operation and therefore change the final answer.

# Slide 14 — BODMAS Ladder

## Mechanic

Students arrange the BODMAS operation groups in the correct order.

Correct order:

1. `B` — Brackets
2. `O` — Of, meaning multiply
3. `D/M` — Division and Multiplication
4. `A/S` — Addition and Subtraction

Teaching note: `D/M` are solved left to right within their group. `A/S` are also solved left to right within their group.

---

# Slide 15 — Nested Brackets Challenge

## Purpose

Students solve expressions with one bracketed expression inside another bracketed expression. The key mechanic is to solve the innermost brackets first, then work outward.

| Question | Expression | Step Sequence | Final Answer |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | `(2 + (3 × 4)) − 5` | `3 × 4 = 12`; `2 + 12 = 14`; `14 − 5 = 9` | `9` |
| 2 | `3 × (2 + (8 ÷ 4))` | `8 ÷ 4 = 2`; `2 + 2 = 4`; `3 × 4 = 12` | `12` |

# Slide 16 — Insert the Brackets

## Purpose

Students place brackets into an expression so the expression reaches a target value.

| Puzzle | Starting Expression | Target | Correct Bracket Placement | Working |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `2 + 3 × 4` | `20` | `(2 + 3) × 4` | `5 × 4 = 20` |
| 2 | `8 − 2 × 3` | `18` | `(8 − 2) × 3` | `6 × 3 = 18` |
| 3 | `3 × 4 + 2` | `18` | `3 × (4 + 2)` | `3 × 6 = 18` |
| 4 | `10 − 2 + 3` | `5` | `10 − (2 + 3)` | `10 − 5 = 5` |

# Slide 17 — Final BODMAS Review

## Part A — Rule-by-Rule Operator Selection

Students identify the first operation to solve in each expression.

| Question | Concept | Expression | Correct First Operation | Resulting Answer |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | × before + | `7 + 2 × 3` | `2 × 3` | `13` |
| 2 | × before − | `15 − 2 × 4` | `2 × 4` | `7` |
| 3 | ÷ before + and − | `5 + 12 ÷ 4` | `12 ÷ 4` | `8` |
| 4 | + and − left to right | `11 − 4 + 2` | `11 − 4` | `9` |
| 5 | × and ÷ left to right | `18 ÷ 3 × 2` | `18 ÷ 3` | `12` |
| 6 | Brackets first | `(5 + 3) × 4` | `5 + 3` | `32` |

## Part B — Full BODMAS Step Ordering

Expression: `24 ÷ (6 − 2) + 3 × 2`

Students order the solving steps.

Required first step:

```text
(6 − 2) = 4
```

Then solve the division and multiplication before addition:

```text
24 ÷ 4 = 6
3 × 2 = 6
```

Required final step:

```text
6 + 6 = 12
```

The lesson accepts the division and multiplication middle steps in either order because both must happen before the final addition and do not affect each other in this expression. The ideal BODMAS sequence is brackets, then division/multiplication, then addition/subtraction.

## Final Rule Recap

- Brackets first.
- Of means multiply.
- Division and multiplication come before addition and subtraction.
- Division and multiplication are solved left to right when they appear together.
- Addition and subtraction are solved after division/multiplication.
- Addition and subtraction are solved left to right when they appear together.
